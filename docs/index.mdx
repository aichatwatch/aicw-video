AICW Video is an open-source AI agent and standalone app. It turns a video interview into short clips for social platforms. The clips have captions. You can hide faces and voices in them.

AICW Video runs on your Mac. It has a command line tool, a local web page called the hub, and an MCP server. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the way an AI tool such as Claude Code calls other programs.

## What it does

- **Syncs separate audio tracks.** It finds an external audio file that matches the video and aligns it.
- **Makes captions.** It transcribes speech on your computer. You preview the captions and then render them into the video.
- **Protects faces.** It finds faces on your computer. It blurs them or covers them with emoji.
- **Protects voices.** It can replace the speaker's voice with a computer voice. This feature is alpha.
- **Adds side illustrations.** It can make an illustration for half of the screen or for the whole video. This feature is beta.
- **Suggests clips.** It finds strong ranges in the video. AI scene analysis is optional.
- **Exports social formats.** It renders MP4 files for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Instagram feed and YouTube landscape.
- **Shows a live preview.** You check ranges, captions, crop, privacy and format before you render.

## Status

AICW Video works on macOS today. Windows support is planned. The project uses the MIT license. It has no telemetry.
